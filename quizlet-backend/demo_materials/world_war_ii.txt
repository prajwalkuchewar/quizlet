World War II Overview

World War II was a global conflict from 1939 to 1945. It involved most of the world's nations, grouped into the Allies and the Axis powers.

Key causes included the rise of fascism in Germany and Italy, Japanese militarism in Asia, and the unresolved grievances left by World War I and the Treaty of Versailles.

The war began on 1 September 1939, when Germany invaded Poland. Britain and France declared war on Germany two days later. The attack on Pearl Harbor in December 1941 brought the United States into the war.

Major turning points included the Battle of Stalingrad (1942-1943), the Battle of Midway (1942), and the D-Day landings in Normandy (June 1944).

The war ended in Europe with Germany's surrender in May 1945, and in the Pacific after the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki and Japan's surrender in August 1945.
